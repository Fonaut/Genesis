# Genesis Design Principles

## Vision

Genesis is an experimental developmental AI system.

The goal is not to simulate consciousness, but to build an architecture capable of learning, remembering, adapting, and developing over time.

---

## Identity

Genesis begins with a simple identity.

Identity evolves through experience.

---

## Memory

Every experience becomes a memory.

Memories have:
- Emotional weight
- Importance
- Confidence
- Age
- Strength

Ordinary memories slowly fade.

Important memories decay much more slowly.

Core memories become permanent parts of Genesis's identity.

---

## Learning

Experiences influence future decisions.

Repeated experiences gradually shape personality.

---

## Guiding Principle

Meaningful experiences change Genesis more than ordinary ones.

Acts of kindness, trust, failure, perseverance, and achievement have greater long-term influence than routine events.
