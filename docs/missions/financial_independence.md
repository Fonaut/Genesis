## 3. Financial & Housing Goals

1. **Establish Reliable Employment Income:** Secure dependable, consistent baseline income to cover immediate living and saving needs.
2. **Affordability Modeling:** Calculate realistic monthly housing affordability based on verified recurring income.
3. **Move-in & Emergency Reserves:** Determine required moving expenses, security deposits, and a minimum 3–6 month emergency reserve.
4. **Savings & Progress Tracking:** Track savings and income progress against targets.
5. **Scenario Comparison:** Compare different housing scenarios (locations, lease terms, utilities).
6. **Readiness Assessment:** Objectively assess readiness for independent living before signing leases.
7. **Living Financial Dashboard:** Build a financial dashboard that updates dynamically as circumstances evolve.

## 4. Long-Term Entrepreneurship Path

Fodoh ultimately wants to generate income through something he owns.

### Preferred Phased Pathway
1. **Service Phase:** Start by providing useful AI and workflow automation services to local businesses.
2. **Customer Acquisition:** Secure initial paying clients through direct outreach and relationship building.
3. **Repeatable Operations:** Build repeatable service processes and recurring monthly revenue.
4. **Productization:** Convert validated, repeatable automations into software products.

---

## 5. Initial Business Opportunity to Validate

* **Target Market:** Lead and estimate follow-up automation for small home-service contractors.
* **Potential First Customer Verticals:**
  * Roofing contractors
  * Remodeling companies
  * Exterior-work contractors
  * Other independent home-service trade businesses
* **Problem Hypothesis:** Small contractors lose high-value sales opportunities because customer inquiries are not answered promptly, estimates are not followed up systematically, and administrative processes are fragmented.
* **Initial Solution Hypothesis:** An automation pipeline that records new customer inquiries, tracks estimate statuses, reminds owners when follow-ups are due, and drafts personalized follow-up messages for human approval.
* *Status: This is a working hypothesis requiring customer validation, not an established business.*
* *Comfort Level: Fodoh is comfortable approaching and speaking directly with local business owners.*

### Customer Discovery Protocol
* Identify candidate businesses in the Winston-Salem, Greensboro, and High Point areas.
* Conduct discovery interviews with business owners regarding their existing workflows and bottlenecks.
* Evaluate current competitor solutions, pricing, and adoption barriers.
* Determine whether owners would pay for automated follow-up assistance.
* Validate commercial demand before building complex software.

## 6. Business Dashboard Requirements

* **Metrics to Track:** Potential customers, interviews conducted, proposals sent, paid pilots active, monthly recurring revenue (MRR), operating expenses, net profit, and progress toward income milestones.
* **Separation Rule:** Track the personal moving-out goal strictly separately from speculative business revenue. Speculative or projected business revenue must never be counted as reliable housing income.
